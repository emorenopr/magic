How it works

From your call to a passed inspection

New Jersey requires a permit and a township inspection for most electrical work. We handle all of it.

  1. Call or book

    Tell us what you need. Emergencies get a callback within the hour.

  2. Free estimate

    We visit, check your panel and load, and give you a written, flat-rate price.

  3. Permit

    We file the electrical permit with your township's construction office.

  4. Install

    Licensed electricians do the work, usually in one or two days.

  5. Inspection

    We meet the inspector and hand you the approved paperwork.

Common questions
How much does a panel upgrade cost?

Most 200A residential upgrades in NJ fall between $2,500 and $4,500 including permit and inspection. We give you a fixed price after a free visit.

Do you handle the permit?

Yes. We file the permit, schedule the inspection with your township and are there when the inspector arrives.

Can I get a rebate for an EV charger?

Many NJ utilities and the state offer incentives for Level 2 chargers. We'll tell you which ones apply and help with the paperwork.

Do you work on weekends?

Emergency calls are answered 24/7. Scheduled work runs Monday to Saturday.
